Show official weather warnings (e.g. severe weather warning) in the email briefing.

In SMS and Telegram compact style the warning block starts with ! — everything after it is an official warning, not our own forecast.

  • TS Gewitter
  • FO Hochwasser/Erdrutsch
  • RA Starkregen
  • WG Sturm
  • SN Schneefall
  • AW Lawine
  • AV Lawinenstufe
  • IC Glatteis
  • HT Hitze
  • CD Kälte
  • FR Waldbrand-Gefahr
  • AB Zugang gesperrt

The warning level follows the colon: L = yellow, M = orange, H = red (e.g. TH:H = red thunderstorm warning).
