[Help]

Available commands:

  TODAY                 – Weather for today's stage
  TOMORROW              – Weather for tomorrow's stage
  NOW                   – Rain and thunderstorm nowcast for the next ~2h
  STORMS                – Thunderstorm risk for today's stage
  ROUTE [km]            – Rain areas along the rest of the route
  RESTDAY [N]           – Shift stages by N days (default: 1)
  STATUS                – Today and upcoming stages
  PAUSE [2d / 12h]      – Pause briefings for a while
  SKIP                  – Skip the next briefing
  STOP                  – Turn briefings off for good (also CANCEL)
  RESUME                – Turn briefings back on
  HELP                  – Show this help
  CODES                 – Show what the codes mean
  STARTDATE YYYY-MM-DD  – Set the trip start date and shift all stages
  REPORT [morning / evening] – Send the briefing now (default: morning)

Weather values (send a code → hourly values):

  Temp / T         – Temperature (°C)
  Night            – Night low (°C)
  DayMin / L       – Daytime low (walking hours) (°C)
  DayMax           – Daytime high (walking hours) (°C)
  Feels / TF       – Feels-like temperature (°C)
  NightF / FN      – Feels-like night low (°C)
  DayMinF / FL     – Feels-like daytime low (walking hours) (°C)
  DayMaxF / FD     – Feels-like daytime high (walking hours) (°C)
  Humid / HU       – Humidity (%)
  Dew / DP         – Dew point (°C)
  Wind / W         – Wind (km/h)
  Gust / G         – Wind gusts (km/h)
  WDir / WD        – Wind direction (°)
  Rain / R         – Precipitation (mm)
  Rain% / PR       – Rain probability (%)
  Thdr / TH        – Thunderstorm
  SnowL / SL       – Snow line (m)
  PType / PT       – Precipitation type
  Cloud / CT       – Cloud cover (%)
  CldLow / CL      – Low clouds (%)
  CldMid / CM      – Mid-level clouds (%)
  CldHi / CH       – High clouds (%)
  Visib / VS       – Visibility (km)
  Sun / SU         – Sunshine hours (h)
  UV               – UV index
  Press / HP       – Air pressure (hPa)
  0°Line / FZ      – Freezing level (m)
  SnowH / SD       – Snow depth (cm)
  NewSn / NS       – Fresh snow (cm)
